# SunStay Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

**SunStay** is an internal hotel management system for **Hotel Tropical Sun**, located in Trinidad, Beni, Bolivia.

The system is designed to centralize and organize administrative and operational hotel processes, including reservations, guests, rooms, billing, inventory, staff attendance, common areas, reports, users, and roles.

SunStay is not a public guest booking website. Guests do not directly interact with the system. Hotel staff manage all guest-related operations.

---

## 2. Business Goals

SunStay must support the following business goals:

- Centralize hotel operational information in a single platform.
- Improve reservation, guest, room, billing, inventory, staff, common area, and reporting management.
- Reduce dependency on manual records and scattered information channels.
- Improve operational control and administrative visibility.
- Support management decision-making through dashboards and reports.
- Provide role-based access to internal hotel users.

---

## 3. User Roles

The system must support the following internal user roles:

### 3.1 Receptionist

The receptionist manages front-desk operations, including reservations, guests, room assignment, check-in, check-out, billing, and common area reservations.

### 3.2 Administrator

The administrator manages system configuration, rooms, staff, users, roles, permissions, common areas, and operational supervision.

### 3.3 Inventory Manager

The inventory manager manages products, inventory areas, stock levels, inventory movements, and low-stock alerts.

### 3.4 Management

Management users consult dashboards, reports, indicators, monthly performance, occupancy, billing, inventory, staff, and operational summaries.

---

## 4. Business Rules

### 4.1 Reservations

- A reservation must have one responsible person or reservation holder.
- A reservation may include one or more guests.
- A reservation may include one or more rooms depending on room type, capacity, and availability.
- A room cannot be assigned to overlapping confirmed reservations for the same date range.
- Reservation status must be tracked.
- Reservation status changes must be traceable.
- A reservation may generate a stay.
- A reservation may generate an invoice.

### 4.2 Guests

- Guests are registered and managed by internal hotel staff.
- A guest may participate in multiple reservations over time.
- A guest may be linked to common area reservations.
- Guest history must be available for operational review.

### 4.3 Rooms

- Each room belongs to a hotel.
- Each room belongs to one room type.
- Each room has a current status.
- Room status history must be traceable.
- Room availability must consider reservations, stays, maintenance, and cleaning states.

### 4.4 Billing

- An invoice must be linked to a reservation.
- An invoice may contain one or more invoice details.
- An invoice must have a payment status.
- An invoice must register a payment method when payment information is recorded.
- The system must calculate subtotals and totals.

### 4.5 Inventory

- Inventory must be organized by hotel areas.
- Inventory areas include breakfast, pool, room supplies, and cleaning products.
- Products must belong to a product category.
- Products must have minimum stock values.
- Stock movements must register entries and exits.
- Each inventory movement must identify the staff member responsible for the operation.
- The system must show low-stock alerts.

### 4.6 Staff and Attendance

- Staff members must have a position and shift.
- Staff daily check-in and check-out must be recorded.
- Attendance history must be available.
- Staff access movements must be traceable.
- Attendance statuses may include present, absent, late, permission, and completed shift.

### 4.7 Common Areas

- Common areas include pool and meeting room.
- Common area availability must be controlled by date and time.
- A common area reservation must be linked to a guest.
- Common area reservations must register start time, end time, status, and observations.

### 4.8 Reports

- Reports must be generated by authorized users.
- Reports must register report type, generation date, date range, format, criteria, and observations.
- Reports must support operational and management analysis.

### 4.9 Users and Access Control

- Internal users must authenticate before accessing the system.
- Users must have roles.
- Role-based access control must restrict module access.
- Users must only access modules and actions allowed by their role.

## 10. Users, Roles, and Module Access Control Update

The creation of the **Users and Roles** module is required because SunStay controls access to system modules according to internal user roles. The system must allow the Administrator to manage users, assign roles, control account status, and define access permissions from the web interface.

### 10.1 Updated business rules

- Every internal user must authenticate before using the system.
- Every user must have an assigned role.
- Only Administrator users can manage users, roles, and permissions.
- Users must only see sidebar modules authorized by their role.
- Backend endpoints must validate permissions before executing protected actions.
- User account statuses must include Active, Inactive, and Blocked.
- Inactive or blocked users must not be allowed to access internal modules.
- Passwords must never be stored as plain text.
- User, role, permission, and account status changes must be traceable.
